How OTF Works

Staff help you through creative roadblocks in Discord—for free. Full end-to-end projects can be hired at sliding-scale rates (half barter/half pay available). Our goal: free workshops, resources, and a safe space for community-to-community support.

Free Discord Help

Staff work through roadblocks with you—quick suggestions, paths you can take, troubleshooting. This keeps things manageable (no burnout!) and leaves energy for OTF TV content. Not full projects, just enough to unstick you.

Community Project Lottery

Want a full project done for free? We pick one community project from a lottery to work on end-to-end. Once it's finished, we pick another one. Submit your project idea in Discord for a chance to be selected.

Projects can be anything: building your portfolio site, editing your podcast season, designing a zine, producing a short film, etc. This keeps the community engaged and gives staff real portfolio work.

Paid Project Work

Free Discord help covers quick troubleshooting and brainstorming. The lottery covers one community project at a time.

Need a project done now? Full end-to-end work (building a website, producing a video series, designing a brand kit, editing a podcast season) can be hired at sliding-scale rates.

Half barter/half pay available in some cases. We prioritize paid roles for community members whenever budgets allow.

Our Mission

Bring free workshops, resources, and materials to neurodivergent & chronically ill creatives. Create a safe space for community-to-community help. Design everything around hyperfocus, energy windows, and care—not deadlines that burn people out.

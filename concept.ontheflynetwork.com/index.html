On The Fly Network Concept

Writing

For Community Members

  • Connect & Collaborate: Find your writing tribe & collaborate across genres. Share your work, get feedback, & participate in challenges. Get support from volunteers & the community.
  • Learn & Grow: Access free resources & workshops. Get expert feedback & guidance. Connect with published authors & industry pros.
  • Bring Your Stories to Life: Polish your writing & find your voice. Learn about Copyrighting/Publishing. Showcase your work & reach new readers.

For Volunteers

  • Share Your Expertise: Mentor and guide aspiring writers. Lead workshops and provide feedback. Foster a vibrant community through engagement.
  • Shape the Community: Build a welcoming and supportive environment. Help expand our resources through partnerships. Foster a love for storytelling.
  • Bring Original Content to Life: Collaborate on On The Fly Network projects. Contribute to podcasts, blog posts, and more.
  • Gain Experience & Recognition: Build your portfolio and network. Develop valuable skills and receive recognition. Potential for paid staff positions in the future.

Photography

For Community Members

  • Connect & Collaborate: Find your photography tribe & connect with fellow photographers of all skill levels. Share your work, receive feedback, & participate in photo challenges. Collaborate on projects, exchange ideas, & find inspiration. Get support from volunteers & the community.
  • Learn & Grow: Access free resources & workshops. Receive expert feedback & personalized guidance. Connect with experienced photographers & industry professionals. Explore various genres & styles. Learn about composition, lighting, editing techniques, and more.
  • Bring Your Vision to Life: Enhance your technical skills and develop your unique artistic vision. Build a stunning portfolio. Network with other photographers and industry professionals. Gain confidence and exposure. Turn your passion for photography into a fulfilling career.

For Volunteers

  • Share Your Expertise: Mentor & inspire fellow photographers. Lead workshops & share your expertise. Foster a welcoming & supportive community.
  • Empower the Community: Contribute to building a thriving photography hub. Curate and share free resources. Provide images for workshops across all creative categories.
  • Inspire Creativity: Offer insights into photo analysis and interpretation. Spark discussions about the emotions and stories behind images. Encourage experimentation with different techniques and styles.
  • Grow & Gain Recognition: Build your portfolio & network. Develop valuable skills & receive recognition. Potential for paid staff positions in the future.

Audio

For Community Members

  • Connect & Create: Find your audio community & collaborate across podcasts, radio dramas, and music creation. Share your work, get feedback, & participate in challenges. Get support from volunteers & the community.
  • Learn & Grow: Access free resources & workshops on audio production, storytelling, and music composition. Get expert feedback & guidance. Connect with experienced podcasters, musicians, and industry pros.
  • Bring Your Audio to Life: Polish your recordings & find your unique sound. Learn about copyright and licensing. Access a library of copyright-free music and sound effects. Showcase your work & reach new listeners.

For Volunteers

  • Share Your Expertise: Mentor and guide aspiring podcasters, musicians, and radio drama creators. Lead workshops. Provide feedback and offer constructive criticism. Foster a vibrant community.
  • Shape the Community: Build a welcoming and supportive environment. Help expand resources through partnerships. Foster a love for audio storytelling and music creation.
  • Bring Original Content to Life: Collaborate on On The Fly Network audio productions. Contribute to podcasts, create sound effects, or compose music.
  • Gain Experience & Recognition: Build your portfolio and network. Gain valuable experience producing, editing, and mentoring. Receive recognition. Potential to transition into paid staff positions as the network grows.

Film/TV

For Community Members

  • Connect & Collaborate: Find like-minded filmmakers, writers, actors, and crew. Pitch your project ideas and find collaborators. Get feedback and receive constructive criticism. Network and build relationships within the industry.
  • Learn & Grow: Access free resources and tools. Participate in workshops and masterclasses. Get mentorship and guidance from experienced filmmakers.
  • Bring Your Vision to Life: Turn your scripts into reality with community support. Showcase your finished projects and receive recognition.

For Volunteers

  • Share Your Expertise: Offer your skills and knowledge. Lead workshops or provide mentorship. Review scripts and provide feedback. Assist with production and post-production tasks.
  • Shape the Community: Build a thriving and supportive environment. Help develop resources and programs. Be a part of something bigger than yourself.
  • Bring Original Content to Life: Collaborate on On The Fly Network productions. Contribute to podcasts, short DIY videos, and other resources.
  • Gain Experience and Recognition: Build your portfolio and network. Gain valuable experience working on real projects. Receive recognition. Potential to transition into paid staff positions when the network becomes a non-profit.
